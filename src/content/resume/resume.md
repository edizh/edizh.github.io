summary: >-
  I am a photonics scientist with 10+ years of experience developing optical sensors and nanophotonic devices, from design and simulation through cleanroom process development to experimental characterization. 

  My technical expertise includes optical simulation (FDTD, FEM), nanofabrication (EBL, MLA, RIE, PVD, ALD), structural characterization (SEM, AFM), and spectroscopic characterization (Raman, infrared absorption, fluorescence, scattering). I have worked extensively with plasmonic metasurfaces, 2D materials, and bulk optical sensors, including fiber-coupled and FTIR-based technologies. Throughout my work I have collaborated with industry partners to develop more scalable fabrication methods for existing optical sensor technologies.

  My research has resulted in several peer-reviewed publications, including multiple articles in ACS Nano and ACS Sensors. I also co-organize ICFO's Nanofabrication Seminar Series and contribute to initiatives aimed at improving experimental reproducibility.
